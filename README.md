#   My Favorite Series
## 1. Demon Slayer

<img width="510" height="580" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3d3ee7fd-0b59-4be4-8c77-612500d263c4" />


*In Taisho-era Japan, Tanjiro Kamado is a kindhearted boy who makes a living selling charcoal. However, his peaceful life is shattered when a Demon slaughters his entire family. His little sister Nezuko is the only survivor, but she has been transformed into a Demon herself! Tanjiro sets out on a dangerous journey to find a way to return his sister to normal and destroy the Demon who ruined his life.*

**Demon Slayer Season Guide**

**The Demon Slayer series is structured into four main seasons, each covering a different arc from the manga. Here's a brief guide to the seasons and their arcs:**

 * **Season 1** Unwavering Resolve Arc - Introduces Tanjiro Kamado and his journey as a demon slayer.

 * **Season 2** Mugen Train Arc and Entertainment District Arc - Continues Tanjiro's adventures with the Mugen Train and the Entertainment District.

 * **Season 3** Swordsmith Village Arc - Explores the hidden Swordsmith Village and the Love and Mist Hashira.

 * **Season 4** Hashira Training Arc - Focuses on Tanjiro's training with the Hashira and the final battle against Muzan.

<img width="480" height="570" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5ac5745a-1603-4ef8-9c56-e7d763eebe0f" />

*Bleach follows teenager Ichigo Kurosaki, whose ability to see ghosts leads him to gain Soul Reaper powers and defend humans from Hollows while guiding souls to the afterlife. It is praised for its memorable characters, striking visual style, dynamic sword combat, and standout arcs like Soul Society, though pacing slows with filler. Widely regarded as a shonen classic, it offers high entertainment for fans of supernatural action.*
